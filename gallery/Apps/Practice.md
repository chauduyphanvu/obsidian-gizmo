---
app: Log how long I practise and see this week against the last and the trend toward my goal
use: "[[Tracker kit]]"
with: {what: practice, unit: minutes, goal: 30}
---
Log the minutes each time you practise an instrument, a language or a sport; the week's average
session is compared with last week's, and the chart draws your goal as a dashed line. To track
something else, change `what`, `unit` and `goal` in `with`. Fix a typo by editing the entry in the
table. It asks for nothing; entries are saved in Practice data. The whole app is the Tracker kit.
