---
app: Log how long I sleep and see whether I'm getting my eight hours
use: "[[Tracker kit]]"
with: {what: sleep, unit: hours, goal: 8}
---
Each morning, log how many hours you slept (and a note on why, if it was a bad night); the week's
average is compared with last week's, and the chart draws eight hours as a dashed line. It asks for
nothing; entries are saved in Sleep data. The whole app is the Tracker kit, which Weight uses too.
