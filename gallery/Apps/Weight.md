---
app: Log my weight and watch the trend toward my goal
use: "[[Tracker kit]]"
with: {what: weight, unit: kg, goal: 75}
---
Log your weight when you weigh in; the week's average is compared with last week's, and the chart
draws your goal as a dashed line (change `goal` and `unit` in `with` to suit). Fix a typo by editing
the entry in the table. It asks for nothing; entries are saved in Weight data. The whole app is the
Tracker kit.
